NOM

XmScrollBar — La classe de widget ScrollBar

SYNOPIS

#include <Xm/ScrollBar.h>

DESCRIPTION

Le widget ScrollBar permet à l'utilisateur de visualiser des données trop volumineuses pour être affichées en une seule fois. Les ScrollBars sont généralement situés à l'intérieur d'une ScrolledWindow et adjacents au widget qui contient les données à visualiser. Lorsque l'utilisateur interagit avec le ScrollBar, les données dans l'autre widget défilent.

Un ScrollBar se compose de deux flèches placées à chaque extrémité d'un rectangle. Ce rectangle est appelé la région de défilement. Un rectangle plus petit, appelé le curseur, est placé à l'intérieur de la région de défilement. Les données défilent en cliquant sur l'une des flèches, en sélectionnant la région de défilement ou en faisant glisser le curseur. Lorsqu'une flèche est sélectionnée, le curseur dans la région de défilement est déplacé dans la direction de la flèche d'une quantité fournie par l'application. Si le bouton de la souris est maintenu enfoncé, le curseur continue de se déplacer à un rythme constant.

Le ratio de la taille du curseur à la taille de la région de défilement correspond généralement à la relation entre la taille des données visibles et la taille totale des données. Par exemple, si 10 % des données sont visibles, le curseur occupe généralement 10 % de la région de défilement. Cela fournit à l'utilisateur un indice visuel sur la taille des données invisibles.

Si le parent du ScrollBar est une ScrolledWindow automatique, le valeur par défaut de XmNtraversalOn est True. Sinon, la valeur par défaut est False.

ScrollBar détient les traits XmQTnavigator.

Classes

ScrollBar hérite du comportement, des ressources et des traits des classes Core et XmPrimitive.

Le pointeur de classe est xmScrollBarWidgetClass.

Le nom de la classe est XmScrollBar.

Specifies the distance over which the scrollbar slider snaps back to its original position when the user drags the mouse outside the ScrollBar edge. This distance is defined in terms of multiples of the width of the slider. Whenever the slider snaps back, the ScrollBar dragCallback is called if there is one.
The default value is large enough to prevent unwanted snapBack activity if the mouse is moved within the boundaries of any reasonable screen. To reset the default, set this resource to a large value, such as 10000.

Specifies the list of callbacks that is called when the user takes an action that moves the slider to the end of the ScrollBar with the maximum value. The reason passed to the callback is XmCR_TO_BOTTOM.
XmNtoTopCallback
Specifies the list of callbacks that is called when the user takes an action that moves the slider to the end of the ScrollBar with the minimum value. The reason passed to the callback is XmCR_TO_TOP.
XmNtroughColor
Specifies the color of the slider trough. This color defaults to the color used for selections.
XmNvalue
Specifies the slider's position, between XmNminimum and (XmNmaximum - XmNsliderSize). The value is constrained to be within these inclusive bounds. The initial value of this resource is the larger of 0 (zero) and XmNminimum.
XmNvalueChangedCallback
Specifies the list of callbacks that is called when the slider is released after being dragged. These callbacks are also called in place of XmNincrementCallback, XmNdecrementCallback, XmNpageIncrementCallback, XmNpageDecrementCallback, XmNtoTopCallback, or XmNtoBottomCallback when one of these callback lists would normally be called but the value of the corresponding resource is NULL. The reason passed to the callback is XmCR_VALUE_CHANGED.

NAME

XmScrollBar - scroll bar widget

SYNOPSIS

#include <Xm/ScrollBar.h>

XmScrollBar widget behavior and usage:

        Composite
                Primitive

XmCreateScrollBar(Widget parent, char *name, ArgList arglist, Cardinal arg_count);

DESCRIPTION

The scroll bar widget provides a mechanism for the user to control the viewing area of another widget. It allows the user to scroll vertically, horizontally, or both.

The scroll bar is composed of a slider, two arrows at each end of the scroll bar, and a trough.

The scroll bar widget is a Primitive widget. It takes part in the XmNscreen, XmNcolormap, XmNvisual, XmNdepth, XmNborder, XmNbackground, XmNforeground, XmNfont, XmNparent, XmNchildren, XmNnum_children, XmNwindow, XmNdestroyCallback, XmNwidth, XmNheight, XmNx, XmNy, XmNtop_shadow_color, XmNbottom_shadow_color, XmNtop_shadow_pixmap, XmNbottom_shadow_pixmap, XmNhighlight_color, XmNhighlight_thickness, XmNshadow_thickness, XmNinitialResourcesPersistent, XmNmappedWhenManaged, XmNsensitive, XmNtranslations.

A ScrollBar widget is created using the XmCreateScrollBar function.

ACTION ROUTINES

The ScrollBar action routines are

CancelDrag():
If the key press occurs during scrolling, cancels the scroll and returns the slider to its previous location in the scrollbar, otherwise, and if the parent is a manager, it passes the event to the parent.
IncrementDownOrRight(Down|Right):
With an argument of Down, or 0 (zero) for compatibility, moves the slider down by one increment. With an argument of Right, or 1 for compatibility, it moves the slider right by one increment. If XmNprocessingDirection is XmMAX_ON_RIGHT or XmMAX_ON_BOTTOM, movement toward the right or bottom calls the callbacks for XmNincrementCallback. If XmNprocessingDirection is XmMAX_ON_LEFT or XmMAX_ON_TOP, movement toward the right or bottom calls the callbacks for XmNdecrementCallback.
IncrementUpOrLeft(Up|Left):
With an argument of Up, or 0 (zero) for compatibility, moves the slider up by one increment. With an argument of Left, or 1 for compatibility, it moves the slider left by one increment. If XmNprocessingDirection is XmMAX_ON_RIGHT or XmMAX_ON_BOTTOM, movement toward the left or top calls the callbacks for XmNdecrementCallback. If XmNprocessingDirection is XmMAX_ON_LEFT or XmMAX_ON_TOP, movement toward the left or top calls the callbacks for XmNincrementCallback.
Moved():
If the button is pressed and the pointer moves, the slider moves with the pointer.
PageDownOrRight(Down|Right):
With an argument of Down or Right, or 0 or 1 for compatibility, moves the slider down or right by one page increment. If XmNprocessingDirection is XmMAX_ON_RIGHT or XmMAX_ON_BOTTOM, movement toward the right or bottom calls the callbacks for XmNpageIncrementCallback. If XmNprocessingDirection is XmMAX_ON_LEFT or XmMAX_ON_TOP, movement toward the right or bottom calls the callbacks for XmNpageDecrementCallback.
PageUpOrLeft(Up|Left):
With an argument of Up or Left, or 0 or 1 for compatibility, moves the slider up or left by one page increment. If XmNprocessingDirection is XmMAX_ON_RIGHT or XmMAX_ON_BOTTOM, movement toward the left or top calls the callbacks for XmNpageDecrementCallback. If XmNprocessingDirection is XmMAX_ON_LEFT or XmMAX_ON_TOP, movement toward the left or top calls the callbacks for XmNpageIncrementCallback.
PrimitiveHelp():
Invokes the help action of the parent Primitive widget.
PrimitiveNextTabGroup():
Invokes the next tab group action of the parent Primitive widget.
PrimitiveParentActivate():
Invokes the Activate action of the parent Primitive widget.
PrimitivePrevTabGroup():
Invokes the previous tab group action of the parent Primitive widget.
Release():
Releases the mouse button.
Select():
Selects the slider or arrow.
TopOrBottom():
Scrolls the slider to the top or bottom of the scroll bar.

XmScrollBar(3)

SYNOPSIS


#include <Xm/ScrollBar.h>

DESCRIPTION

The XmScrollBar widget provides a graphical representation of a scroll bar. It allows the user to select a value within a range by moving a slider. The widget consists of a slider, two arrows at either end, and a region between the arrows. The value represented by the slider is determined by its position within the scroll bar.

When the user interacts with the scroll bar, the widget performs actions that can trigger callbacks. These actions and their corresponding callbacks are described below.

DecrementOrLeft(Down|Left):
With an argument of Down, or 0 (zero) for compatibility, moves the slider down by one increment. With an argument of Left, or 1 for compatibility, it moves the slider left by one increment. If XmNprocessingDirection is XmMAX_ON_RIGHT or XmMAX_ON_BOTTOM, movement to the left or top calls the callbacks for XmNincrementCallback. If XmNprocessingDirection is XmMAX_ON_LEFT or XmMAX_ON_TOP, movement to the left or top calls the callbacks for XmNdecrementCallback. The XmNvalueChangedCallback is called if the XmNincrementCallback or XmNdecrementCallback is NULL.
IncrementUpOrLeft(Up|Left):
With an argument of Up, or 0 (zero) for compatibility, moves the slider up by one increment. With an argument of Left, or 1 for compatibility, it moves the slider left by one increment. If XmNprocessingDirection is XmMAX_ON_RIGHT or XmMAX_ON_BOTTOM, movement to the left or top calls the callbacks for XmNdecrementCallback. If XmNprocessingDirection is XmMAX_ON_LEFT or XmMAX_ON_TOP, movement to the left or top calls the callbacks for XmNincrementCallback. The XmNvalueChangedCallback is called if the XmNincrementCallback or XmNdecrementCallback is NULL.
Moved():
If the button press occurs within the slider, the subsequent motion events move the slider to the position of the pointer and call the callbacks for XmNdragCallback.
PageDownOrRight(Down|Right):
With an argument of Down, or 0 (zero) for compatibility, moves the slider down by one page increment. With an argument of Right, or 1 for compatibility, moves the slider right by one page increment. If XmNprocessingDirection is XmMAX_ON_RIGHT or XmMAX_ON_BOTTOM, movement toward the right or bottom calls the callbacks for XmNpageIncrementCallback. If XmNprocessingDirection is XmMAX_ON_LEFT or XmMAX_ON_TOP, movement toward the right or bottom calls the XmNpageDecrementCallback callbacks. The XmNvalueChangedCallback is called if the XmNpageIncrementCallback or XmNpageDecrementCallback is NULL.
PageUpOrLeft(Up|Left):
With an argument of Up, or 0 (zero) for compatibility, moves the slider up by one page increment. With an argument of Left, or 1 for compatibility, it moves the slider left by one page increment. If XmNprocessingDirection is XmMAX_ON_RIGHT or XmMAX_ON_BOTTOM, movement to the left or top calls the callbacks for XmNpageDecrementCallback. If XmNprocessingDirection is XmMAX_ON_LEFT or XmMAX_ON_TOP, movement to the left or top calls the XmNpageIncrementCallback callbacks. The XmNvalueChangedCallback is called if the XmNpageIncrementCallback or XmNpageDecrementCallback is NULL.
PrimitiveHelp():
Calls the callbacks for XmNhelpCallback if any exist. If there are no help callbacks for this widget, this action calls the help callbacks for the nearest ancestor that has them.
PrimitiveNextTabGroup():
Traverses to the first item in the next tab group. If the current tab group is the last entry in the tab group list, it wraps to the beginning of the tab group list.
PrimitiveParentActivate():
If the parent is a manager, passes the event to the parent.
PrimitivePrevTabGroup():
Traverses to the first item in the previous tab group. If the beginning of the tab group list is reached, it wraps to the end of the tab group list.
Release():
If the button press occurs within the slider and the slider position is changed, the callbacks for XmNvalueChangedCallback are called.
Select():
In arrow: Moves the slider by one increment in the direction of the arrow. If XmNprocessingDirection is XmMAX_ON_RIGHT or XmMAX_ON_BOTTOM, movement toward the right or bottom calls the callbacks for XmNincrementCallback, and movement to the left or top calls the callbacks for XmNdecrementCallback. If XmNprocessingDirection is XmMAX_ON_LEFT or XmMAX_ON_TOP, movement toward the right or bottom calls the callbacks for XmNdecrementCallback, and movement to the left or top calls the callbacks for XmNincrementCallback. The XmNvalueChangedCallback is called if the XmNincrementCallback or XmNdecrementCallback is NULL.
In scroll region between an arrow and the slider: Moves the slider by one page increment in the direction of the arrow. If XmNprocessingDirection is XmMAX_ON_RIGHT or XmMAX_ON_BOTTOM, movement toward the right or bottom calls the callbacks for XmNpageIncrementCallback, and movement to the left or top calls the callbacks for XmNpageDecrementCallback. If XmNprocessingDirection is XmMAX_ON_LEFT or XmMAX_ON_TOP, movement toward the right or bottom calls the callbacks for XmNpageDecrementCallback, and movement to the left or top calls the callbacks for XmNpageIncrementCallback. The XmNvalueChangedCallback is called if the XmNpageIncrementCallback or XmNpageDecrementCallback is NULL.
In slider: Activates the interactive dragging of the slider.
If the button is held down in either the arrows or the scroll region longer than the XmNinitialDelay resource, the slider is moved again by the same increment and the same callbacks are called. After the initial delay has been used, the time delay changes to the time defined by the resource XmNrepeatDelay.
TopOrBottom():
<Ctrl><Btn1Down> in an arrow or in the scroll region between an arrow and the slider moves the slider as far as possible in the direction of the arrow. If XmNprocessingDirection is XmMAX_ON_RIGHT or XmMAX_ON_BOTTOM, movement toward the right or bottom calls the callbacks for XmNtoBottomCallback, and movement to the left or top calls the callbacks for XmNtoTopCallback. If XmNprocessingDirection is XmMAX_ON_LEFT or XmMAX_ON_TOP, movement toward the right or bottom calls the callbacks for XmNtoTopCallback, and movement to the left or top calls the callbacks for XmNtoBottomCallback. The XmNvalueChangedCallback is called if the XmNtoTopCallback or XmNtoBottomCallback is NULL. Pressing <Key><osfBeginLine> or <Key><osfBeginData> moves the slider to the minimum value and invokes the XmNtoTopCallback. Pressing <Key><osfEndLine> or <Key><osfEndData> moves the slider to the maximum value and invokes the XmNtoBottomCallback.
