Modèles Strftime

%c (%a %b %e %H:%M:%S %Y) - format date heure

 2008-02-05T18:30:30 = lun. févr.  5 18:30:30 2008
 1995-12-22T09:05:02 = ven. déc. 22 09:05:02 1995
-0010-09-15T04:44:23 = sam. sept. 15 04:44:23 -10

 2008-02-05T18:30:30 = 02/05/08
 1995-12-22T09:05:02 = 12/22/95
-0010-09-15T04:44:23 = 09/15/10

 2008-02-05T18:30:30 = 18:30:30
 1995-12-22T09:05:02 = 09:05:02
-0010-09-15T04:44:23 = 04:44:23
